# Partnerships and services

OpenLegalCore Word Connector is open-source software licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The license permits commercial use, modification,
and distribution; no separate commercial software license is required for this
connector.

The project is designed for organizations that want inspectable legal-AI
integration, explicit document boundaries and operator control over identity,
infrastructure, legal sources and model access.

## Areas for collaboration

Organizations can work with OpenLegalCore on:

- evaluation and integration planning;
- operator-controlled deployment and security review;
- compatible backend and legal-source integration;
- implementation, training, and support; and
- research or product partnerships.

The [technical guide](docs/TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md) explains the connector
architecture, and the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) distinguishes the current beta from
possible future directions. A collaboration does not turn a roadmap direction
into a delivery commitment unless it is covered by a separate written scope.

## Commercial and operational boundary

The source release does not include a hosted OpenLegalCore service, access to
private staging, the OLC Engine, legal databases, service levels, support, or
warranty. Those components and services require separate arrangements when
offered.

A separately operated deployment must define its own identity, hosting,
security, privacy, retention, logging, support and incident-response terms.
The repository's [privacy statement](PRIVACY.md),
[security policy](SECURITY.md) and [support boundary](SUPPORT.md) describe the
source project; they are not substitutes for an operator's service policies.

Apache-2.0 does not grant rights to use OpenLegalCore names or marks as branding
for another product or service. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md). Third-party
material remains under the terms recorded in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Contact

For evaluation, integration, research or service discussions, email
[sales@openlegalcore.org](mailto:sales@openlegalcore.org). A useful first
message identifies the intended Word client, backend, legal-source scope,
deployment model, security constraints and desired outcome without including
client-confidential data or credentials.
